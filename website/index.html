Contribute / research submission

Submit research to the archive

Every officially nominated technique is already here. What the nomination rounds missed is the open question — and that is what this is for. Fill in what you know; the button below opens a prefilled GitHub issue you can read and edit before posting. Nothing leaves this page until you do.

↗ Open the prefilled issue

A GitHub account is needed to post it. Submissions are judged on the evidence — novel method, primary source, verified non-duplicate — and accepted ones join their year under “Missed from the original list”. Submitting your own research is welcome and judged no differently.
